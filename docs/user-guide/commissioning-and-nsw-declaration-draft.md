# Commissioning and the NSW declaration draft

Both appear on the Review screen once the package is complete.

## Commissioning sheets

Available when all three gates are signed. Download XLSX or PDF: one row per terminal with its design airflow from the signed revision, and blank measured columns for the technician.

To import readings:

1. Enter a tolerance in percent. You decide it: the app assumes none. It must be a number from 0 to 50.
2. Upload the filled-in XLSX.
3. The table shows design, measured, variance and a flag per terminal, plus counts.

Readings are records, not rule results. The import never changes the signed package.

## NSW design compliance declaration (draft)

Shown for NSW projects of class 2, 3 and 9c, once all three gates are signed.

1. Click "Prepare the draft". The page shows the building, the gate signers with registration numbers, the accepted FAILs and the Performance Solutions recorded.
2. Read the list of fields the practitioner must complete.
3. "Open the draft PDF" produces the draft.

The registered design practitioner reviews, completes and lodges it on the NSW Planning Portal. This app lodges nothing, and the draft is not a declaration. Its wording and format are placeholders pending legal review.

## What the app will refuse

- Commissioning sheets or the declaration draft before all three gates are signed.
- A commissioning import without a valid tolerance, or with a file that is not an XLSX from the sheet.
- Preparing the declaration for a project that is not in NSW.
