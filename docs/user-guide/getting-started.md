# Getting started

## Signing in

Sign in with the magic link sent to your e-mail address. People join a firm by invitation only: the firm administrator invites an address, and you join once that address is confirmed. If you have no firm, the app shows the join screen instead of the application. If your administrator has deactivated your account you see "Account deactivated" and nothing else works.

## Roles

The header shows who you are and your role. The server enforces every permission; the header is a display.

- Designer: uploads models, confirms Gate 1 inputs, runs rules, freezes a revision (this is the designer's sign-off for Gate 1).
- Checker: reviews results and signs Gate 2. The checker must be a different person from the one who froze the revision, and must have reviewed at least one line personally.
- Approver: acknowledges accepted FAILs and signs Gate 3 with a verified registration number.
- Firm administrator: invites people, sets roles, sets firm settings, uploads title block and layer standards.

If you hold more than one role you can switch with the "acting as" selector. In a small firm one person may hold all three gates; the header then shows a red independence notice and everything produced is stamped NOT INDEPENDENTLY CHECKED. See "Review and sign-off".

## Projects and revisions

Open Projects to see the dashboard. You can filter by state, NCC edition and status, and search. Creating a project is billing-gated (new projects only: signed work is never locked).

A project has one NCC edition. NCC 2022 and NCC 2025 rules are separate and are never mixed in one evaluation.

Each architect model issue is a revision (A, B, ...). Open a revision to reach Gate 1, the results, the diff, review, services, drafting, preview and Performance Solution pages.

## What the app will refuse

- Any action from a deactivated account.
- Any action your role does not allow, with the reason shown.
- Changes to a frozen revision: it can never change; upload a new revision instead.
